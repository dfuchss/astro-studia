Papers

Not every publication needs a page. These are the ones with something more to say than the citation — an abstract, a figure, a replication package. The rest live on publications.

  • LCL

    De Re Publica: the mixed constitution

    Six books on the best form of the state, of which most is lost. What survives argues that a constitution combining monarchy, aristocracy and democracy is more stable than any of the three on its own.

    1928

  • LCL

    De Officiis

    1913

  • CUP

    Laelius de Amicitia

    A dialogue on friendship, written in the same year as De Officiis and set in 129 BC. Laelius, having just lost Scipio, is asked what friendship is for.

    2021 · to appear

  • De legibus naturae: praelectio Latine habita

    A lecture on natural law and the opening books of the De Legibus, given in Latin. The colloquium publishes nothing, so this page is all there is.

    2026 · 🏛 Conventiculum Arpinas
