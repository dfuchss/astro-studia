De Officiis

Marcus Tullius Cicero, and Walter Miller

LCL1913

Cambridge, MA

DOI

Cicero’s last work, written in the months before his death, addressed to his son and structured around a single question: what should one do when the honourable and the expedient appear to conflict?

His answer is that they never actually do — that the conflict is a mistake about what is expedient. The third book is the hard one, and it is mostly worked examples.
