What the programme runs on

Turning Greek philosophy into Latin took more than a translator: a vocabulary that did not exist yet, source texts worth basing a version on, and a way to get the result down as fast as it was dictated.

  • A Latin vocabulary for philosophy

    Greek philosophy had two centuries of technical vocabulary and Latin had almost none. Much of the wordlist still in use was coined, or given its current sense, in the course of translating it.

  • Notae Tironianae

    A shorthand system devised to keep up with dictation and senate debate. Some four thousand signs at first, and still in use as a scribal hand a thousand years later.

  • TL

    The letters, at Perseus

    ↗

    Around nine hundred letters, digitised and cross-linked. The closest thing the ancient world leaves us to a complete inbox.
